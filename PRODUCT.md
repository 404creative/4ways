# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Egyptians buying a home or an investment unit inside Egypt, from Greater Cairo (New Cairo, the New Capital, Sheikh Zayed, 6th of October, Maadi, Mostakbal City) to the coast (North Coast, Ain Sokhna). They compare compounds, developers, prices and payment plans, usually on a phone, often in Egyptian Arabic. The site is buyer-only.

## Product Purpose

Demo website for 4 Ways Real Estate, a real estate marketing company in New Cairo. It lets a buyer search in their own words, browse areas and compounds down to individual units, compare up to three units, check what their budget can buy, see new launches, and reach the team on WhatsApp or by phone at any point.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single static page (`index.html`), Arabic right-to-left, no build step. Forked from the Lynkers demo (`404creative/lynkers`) with the same features.
- Accounts, one-time codes, favorites and leads are demo-only and live in the browser's localStorage. No backend yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: 4 Ways Real Estate (فور وايز). The Arabic spelling is an assumption to confirm with the client.
- Mark: traced to vector from the Facebook logo (`assets/logo-mark.svg`); wordmark in Jost with wide tracking.
- Colors: black, raw concrete greys from the Facebook cover, plus one bronze accent proposed for the demo.
- Lines from their cover: "Building tomorrow's communities", "Build, Invest, Live, Thrive", "Better spaces, stronger futures, lasting value".
- Waysy (وايزي) is the chatbot, renamed from Lynkers' Linky. The name is a demo proposal.
