# Design System: 4 Ways Real Estate

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Trusted Advisor"**

4 Ways should feel like a calm consultant sitting next to the buyer: clear, honest and in no hurry to push. The page is mostly white paper and deep black, with photography of real homes doing the emotional work and the interface staying out of the way. Bronze appears only where the buyer can act or where something is genuinely new, so it keeps its meaning.

Density is moderate. Sections breathe with even spacing, cards sit on thin borders rather than heavy shadows, and every price is shown with its down payment, years and monthly installment so nothing feels hidden. The whole site is Arabic and right-to-left; Kufi headings give it a confident, modern Egyptian voice while the Sans body keeps long text easy to read.

Forked from the Lynkers demo (404creative/lynkers) with the same layout and features. Avoid dark "luxury" gold styling, background patterns, scattered decoration and anything copied from other portals.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White and black foundation; bronze reserved for actions and "new".
- Real property photography carries the mood; no illustrations or textures.
- Thin borders at rest, a soft shadow only on hover.
- One radius family (10 / 12 / 16 / 20px) and pill chips for small filters.
- Arabic RTL, Kufi headings, Sans body, Cormorant only for the "4 Ways" wordmark.

## Colors

A restrained palette taken from the 4 Ways logo and Facebook cover: black does almost all of the work, raw concrete greys support it, and bronze is the single accent added for actions (it is a demo proposal, not from their logo).

### Primary
- **Advisor Black** (#161616): headings, primary buttons, selected chips and tabs, prices, the budget summary card, the inquiry band on the releases page. It is the brand's voice.
- **Advisor Black Light** (#2e2c29): top of the primary button gradient, links and hover states.

### Secondary
- **Bronze** (#b0764a): the logo accent; used for the hand-drawn underline under "مع فور وايز", small decorative dots and map highlights. Never as a fill behind white text.
- **Bronze Ink** (#8c5a33): the accessible bronze for white-on-bronze buttons ("احجز بسعر الإطلاق", "تخطّى"), "new" and "offer" tags, and bronze text such as the hero's "بيتك".

### Tertiary
- **Concrete** (#d8d4cb): prices on dark photo overlays, EOI badges on launch cards.
- **Stone** (#cfccc5): soft brand tint, used sparingly.

### Neutral
- **Ink** (#1c1b19): body text.
- **Ink Soft** (#55524c): secondary text, chip labels, descriptions.
- **Muted** (#6a665f): captions, meta lines, helper text (meets 4.5:1 on white).
- **Paper** (#ffffff): page and card background.
- **Surface Soft** (#f6f5f2): alternating section bands, stat tiles, hero background.
- **Border** (#e7e5e0) and **Border Strong** (#cdcac3): card and input outlines at rest and on hover.
- **Deep Space** (#0b0b0a): only behind the globe in the map intro and the full-screen video viewer, with a light star field and two small planets (planet and star colors are listed in the frontmatter and used nowhere else).
- **Black Tint** (#efede9): icon tiles, developer monograms, count pills.
- **Hover Tint** (#f8f7f5), **Border Input** (#d9d6d0), **Border Hover** (#a5a199): the line button, chips and inputs at rest and on hover.
- **Image Placeholder** (#e2dfd9): behind photos while they load.
- **Concrete tints** (#f5f4f2, #e3e1dd, #eeedea, #f4f3f2 and their lines/text): the releases page header, "إطلاق جديد" badge, EOI chip and master-plan button only.
- **Notice** (#fff8e6 with a dashed #e2bf5f border): the demo-only one-time-code notice.
- **Bronze Tint** (#fdecea) and **OK Tint** (#eaf7f0): the medical service icon and the best value in the comparison table.

Button gradients use the listed top/bottom stops (black, bronze fill, WhatsApp) and darker stops on hover; no other gradients appear on controls.

### Named Rules
**The Bronze Means Act Rule.** Bronze appears on buttons the buyer can press and on "new" or "offer" labels. Prices, numbers and data stay black.

**The Accessible Bronze Rule.** White text never sits on #b0764a; fills behind text use Bronze Ink #8c5a33.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Noto Kufi Arabic (with sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Noto Sans Arabic (with sans-serif)
**Wordmark Font:** Jost, for "4 Ways" only

**Character:** a modern, geometric Kufi for headings that reads as confident and local, paired with a neutral Arabic Sans that stays comfortable for long descriptions and numbers.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp(34px, 4.8vw, 58px), 1.22): the home hero heading only.
- **Headline** (700, 26px, 1.3; 22px on mobile): section titles such as "أهم المناطق", page titles on inner pages up to 48px.
- **Title** (600, 16px, 1.35): card titles, compound and unit names.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.6): default text; descriptions at 15px with 1.9 line-height, max about 620px wide.
- **Lead** (400, 17px, 1.9): hero and page intro paragraphs.
- **Label** (600, 13px): buttons, chips, tabs.
- **Small** (400, 14px) and **Body Small** (400, 15px, 1.9): secondary lines, area and compound descriptions.
- **Caption** (500, 12px) and **Micro** (600, 11px): meta lines, photo badges (12px), counters.
- **Title Large / XL** (700, 18px / 20px): card and dialog titles, launch and release names.
- **Headline Small** (700, 22px) and **Headline Large** (700, clamp(30px, 4.4vw, 48px)): mobile section titles; inner page titles.
- **Price Large** (700, 28px): the starting price in the compound price box.

The ramp is closed: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 20, 22, 26, 28px plus the display and headline clamps. New text uses one of these sizes.

### Named Rules
**The No Eyebrow Rule.** No small label above a heading. The heading carries its own weight.

**The Tabular Price Rule.** Prices, stats and tables use tabular numerals so figures line up.

## Layout

A centered 1200px container with 16px side gutters. Home sections share one rhythm: 56px top and bottom (40px on mobile), alternating white and Surface Soft bands so each section reads on its own without dividers. The section header (title left of its tools) always sits 22px above its content.

The home hero is a split: text and the search card on the reading side, a photo collage led by a large presenter video on the other. Inner pages (area, compound, releases, about, blog) open with a Surface Soft header band that holds breadcrumbs, the title, a short description and key figures, then move to white content. The compound page uses a main column plus a 340px sticky price box. Grids collapse to two columns under 1000px and one column under 640px; nothing may scroll horizontally at 375px.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Surfaces separate through borders and the alternating section bands, not shadows. Shadows are reserved for things that float (the search card, hero images, the sticky price box, modals, the chatbot) and for hover feedback on cards.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Resting** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(22,22,22,.05)`): large containers like the price box.
- **Hover lift** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -14px rgba(22,22,22,.28)`): cards on hover, together with translateY(-2px).
- **Floating** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -28px rgba(22,22,22,.40)`): the hero search card and collage images.

### Named Rules
**The One Elevation Rule.** A card has a border or a shadow, never a wide shadow under a border at rest.

## Shapes

Soft, consistent rounding on a closed scale of 4 / 10 / 12 / 16 / 20px and pills. 4px is only for thin bars (progress, sliders). Small controls and badges use 10px, inputs and buttons 12px, cards 16px, large containers and the search card 20px. Chips, tags and filter buttons are pills (999px). Photos inside cards are clipped to the card radius. Circles are used only for avatars, developer logos and round icon buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 12px radius, 42px tall (34px and 10px radius for the small size, 40px circle for icon buttons).
- **Primary:** Advisor Black with a subtle top-to-bottom gradient (#34322e → #161616), white 14px/600 label, 18px side padding.
- **Accent:** Bronze Ink gradient (#9a6439 → #8c5a33) for booking and launch actions.
- **WhatsApp:** green gradient (#17844f → #136f42) with the WhatsApp icon.
- **Line:** white with a #d9d6d0 border and black label; hover darkens the border and tints the background #f8f7f5.
- **Hover / Focus:** lift 1px on hover, scale to 0.98 on press, 2px #3d3a35 outline with 2px offset on keyboard focus.

### Chips
- **Style:** 34–36px pills, white with a #d9d6d0 border, Ink Soft 13px/600 label, optional bronze icon.
- **State:** selected chips turn Advisor Black with white text and a small count in lighter text.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px.
- **Background:** Paper on Surface Soft bands, Surface Soft tiles inside white areas.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest; Hover lift on hover (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px #e7e5e0, #cdcac3 on hover.
- **Internal Padding:** 14–20px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white, 1px #d9d6d0 border, 12px radius, 46px tall; selects use a custom chevron on the leading side.
- **Focus:** black border plus a 3px rgba(46,44,41,.22) ring.
- **Placeholder:** #716d66, short enough to never be cut off.

### Navigation
- **Style:** a 72px white header, slightly translucent with blur, holding the logo, text links (15px/500, Ink Soft, black when active) and, on the leading side, round account and favorites buttons plus the black "كلّم مستشار" WhatsApp button. "إطلاقات جديدة" carries a small bronze dot. On mobile the links collapse and a fixed bottom bar offers call and WhatsApp.

### Unit card (signature)
Photo with small badges (new, offer, delivery), quick actions for favorite, share and compare, then the developer monogram, area, type and project name, beds/baths/area icons, the monthly installment line, and a price row with round call and WhatsApp buttons.

### Waysy (signature)
The chatbot robot sits at the bottom corner and mostly stays still. It waves, winks, points or looks around every 6–12 seconds; flips and flights are rare. Speech bubbles are white cards with a close button.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep bronze for actions and "new"; show prices and numbers in Advisor Black.
- **Do** use Bronze Ink #8c5a33 behind any white text.
- **Do** keep every card on a 1px #e7e5e0 border with the 16px radius, and add shadow only on hover.
- **Do** show price, down payment, years and monthly installment together, and label demo or estimated values.
- **Do** keep sections on the 56px rhythm and alternate white and #f6f5f2 bands.
- **Do** check every page at 375px for horizontal overflow.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gold, dark "luxury" sections or heavy dark backgrounds (the map's space intro is the only dark surface).
- **Don't** add background patterns, dot grids, blobs, rings or other decoration.
- **Don't** put an eyebrow label above a heading or number sections "01 / 02".
- **Don't** nest a card inside a card or stack many chips on top of a photo.
- **Don't** give each developer a different bright color; monograms stay #efede9 with black letters until real logos arrive.
- **Don't** copy Nawy's or The Village's content, images or logos.
